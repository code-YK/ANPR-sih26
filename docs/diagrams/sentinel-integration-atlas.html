Notation

Colour is spent only on what actually moves

Four hues, total — borrowed from the operator console’s own rule that colour is load-bearing for urgency and is therefore reserved for nothing else. Three of them name the payloads that travel the system; the fourth is everything else.

  • Live video · never reaches the browser raw
  • Observations · plates, tracks, evidence
  • Alerts · a person must act
  • Control · metadata, config, audit

01 · The whole path

A frame crosses five trust boundaries to become an alert

Read top to bottom. The department estate is never dialled into and never reconfigured; Sentinel is a stream consumer with the privileges of any authorised viewer. An operator’s browser has no route back to Zone 1 at all.

02 · The integration ladder

Six ways in, one contract out

Integration is not one problem. Every source is placed on the highest rung it can reach, and the rung decides what the platform can offer for that camera — but all six terminate in the same internal record, so nothing downstream knows or cares which rung a camera came from.

03 · Onboarding

Four stages, each independently re-runnable

A partial estate onboarding can be resumed without redoing successful work, and any stage can fail without corrupting the registry. Every stage is idempotent on camera_id.

04 · The live decision path

Three gates stand between a plate and an interrupted operator

The branches on the right are where most of the design lives. None of them throws data away — each one only decides whether a human is interrupted.
