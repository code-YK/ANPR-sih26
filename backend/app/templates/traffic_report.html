How to read these figures. Trips were split on an idle gap of {{ '%.0f'|format(report.trip_gap_minutes) }} minutes. Onward direction is the straight-line bearing from one camera to the next, not a measured heading at the camera. Minimum average speed is a lower bound: the road distance between two cameras is at least the straight-line distance, so a vehicle cannot have averaged less than the figure shown. It is not the vehicle's speed and must not be used as one — no camera in this registry is calibrated, so speed at a single camera is not derivable at all. A leg flagged implausible indicates a data fault, most often one plate string misread across two vehicles, and never a speeding finding.

Sightings are written once per vehicle track and only once a plate is confirmed, so this report covers plate-readable traffic, not all traffic. Cameras outside the requesting user's authorised departments are excluded before any figure above is computed. First and last observed nodes are the ends of the observed portion of a trip, which begins and ends wherever cameras happen to be — they are not the journey's true origin and destination.
