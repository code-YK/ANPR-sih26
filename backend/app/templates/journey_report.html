Evidence column shows the authenticated API path for each sighting's evidence crop, where retained -- not a public link. Restricted stops (on cameras outside this viewer's authorised departments) are counted above but not listed by row.

Onward direction is the straight-line bearing from one camera to the next, not a measured heading at the camera. Min avg speed is a lower bound: the road distance between two cameras is at least the straight-line distance, so the vehicle cannot have averaged less than the figure shown -- it is not the vehicle's speed and must not be read as one. No camera in this registry is calibrated, so speed at a single camera is not derivable at all.
